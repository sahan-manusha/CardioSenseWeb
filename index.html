About the Research

Addressing the Global CVD Crisis

Cardiovascular diseases (CVDs) remain the world's leading cause of death, claiming 17.9 million lives annually — 32% of all global deaths (WHO, 2023). Early detection and personalised intervention are critical yet remain inaccessible in low-resource healthcare environments.

CardioSense is a comprehensive AI-powered platform that integrates four intelligent components to deliver end-to-end cardiovascular health monitoring — from symptom analysis to personalised lifestyle recommendations.

  • Logistic Regression + TF-IDF risk pipeline — 97.5% accuracy
  • Fine-tuned DenseNet-121 CXR analysis — 14-label Grad-CAM XAI
  • XGBoost + Bootstrap ensemble — 15 personalised recommendations
  • QLoRA fine-tuned TinyLlama-1.1B mental health chatbot
  • Firebase-backed multi-user platform with PDF reporting

Research Components

Four Pillars of Cardiovascular Intelligence

Each component addresses a distinct research gap in cardiovascular healthcare through advanced AI and machine learning

01
IT22126856

Symptom Analysis & CVD Risk Prediction

Hybrid NLP-ML pipeline combining TF-IDF symptom vectorisation with 13 structured clinical features (BP, cholesterol, BMI, lifestyle) via Logistic Regression. Achieves 97.5% accuracy and 98.34% ROC-AUC with SHAP interpretability for each prediction.

Logistic Regression TF-IDF SHAP Scikit-learn
Malalagama D.D.  |  CVD Risk Dataset — 13 clinical features
02
IT22296146

Chest X-Ray Cardiac Analysis

Fine-tuned DenseNet-121 (PyTorch) for 14-label multi-class chest pathology classification including cardiomegaly, pleural effusion, and atelectasis. Integrates Grad-CAM heatmaps for clinical visual explainability with class-balanced training on the CheXpert dataset.

DenseNet-121 Grad-CAM PyTorch 14 Pathologies
Athukorala A.A.S.M.  |  CheXpert Dataset (14-label multi-label)
03
IT22638854

Personalised Cardiovascular Health Recommendations

Calibrated XGBoost risk model (ROC-AUC: 0.7999) combined with a OneVsRest LogReg recommender (mean ROC-AUC: 0.9951) on 68,583 records. Bootstrap ensemble (7 models) generates 15 ranked lifestyle recommendations with counterfactual risk-reduction scoring.

XGBoost Bootstrap Ensemble 15 Recommendations Counterfactual AI
Randini H.M.R.  |  Kaggle CVD Dataset — 68,583 records
04
IT22230706

AI Mental Health Monitoring Chatbot

Custom-trained TinyLlama-1.1B fine-tuned via QLoRA (4-bit, rank=16) on 38,249 mental health conversations. The trained model delivers empathetic cardiac patient support, administers PHQ-9/GAD-7 assessments, and generates risk-tiered clinical alerts.

TinyLlama-1.1B QLoRA Fine-tuned PHQ-9 / GAD-7 38K Training Samples
Kuruneru S.G.  |  MentalChat16K + Empathetic Dialogues + MedQuAD
